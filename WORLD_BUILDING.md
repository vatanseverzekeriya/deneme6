## 🎮 Technical Features

### Tiled Map Editor Integration
- **JSON Export Support**: Maps designed in Tiled can be exported and imported
- **Tile-based Rendering**: 32x32 pixel tiles
- **Collision Detection**: Walls, trees, and obstacles block movement
- **Procedural Generation**: Each map generates unique tile layouts
- **Large Maps**: Up to 200x200 tiles (6400x6400 pixels)

### Camera System
- **Smooth Following**: Camera follows player smoothly
- **World Coordinates**: Converts between world and screen space
- **Viewport Culling**: Only renders visible tiles for performance
- **Infinite Scrolling**: Large maps with seamless exploration

### Boss Mechanics
- **Multi-Phase Battles**: Bosses change behavior at HP thresholds
- **Special Abilities**: Unique attacks and summons
- **Scaling System**: Mini-bosses (1.5-2.0x), Major bosses (2.0-3.5x)
- **Loot Tables**: Guaranteed and rare drops with rarity tiers

### NPC System
- **Dialogue**: Multiple dialogue lines
- **Merchants**: Buy/sell items (interface ready)
- **Quest Givers**: Track objectives and rewards
- **Interaction**: Press F or tap to interact

### Secret Areas
- **Level Requirements**: Unlock at specific levels
- **Hidden Entrances**: Discover by exploring
- **Special Rewards**: Unique items and large gold amounts
- **Guaranteed Loot**: Always get something valuable
